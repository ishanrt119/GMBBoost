# GBP Scoring & Intelligence Models

## 1. GBP Health Score V2 (Phase 6)

The primary metric of the audit is the GBP Health Score (0–100). It is a composite index calculated from 7 weighted pillars:

1.  **SEO Score**: Keyword optimization in description, services, and profile text.
2.  **Review Score**: Quantity, quality, and recency of reviews.
3.  **Authority Score**: Market dominance compared to local tier benchmarks.
4.  **Engagement Score**: Post frequency, Q&A activity, and owner response rates.
5.  **Completeness Score**: Utilization of all available GBP attributes and fields.
6.  **Visibility Score**: Map ranking performance across the local grid.
7.  **Conversion Score**: Presence of booking links, high-quality photos, and direct CTAs.

*Note: Specific weightings will be tuned dynamically based on the primary category's industry standards.*

## 2. Competitor Gap Analysis (Phase 7)

This module generates a direct comparison: **You vs. Best Competitor**.
Metrics compared visually:
*   Total Reviews & Average Rating
*   Categories & Services utilized
*   Photo Count & Post Frequency
*   Review Velocity & Response Rate

## 3. Keyword Gap Analysis (Phase 8)

Identifies high-value local keywords that competitors are ranking for in the Local Pack, but the target business is missing.
*   **Output Matrix**: Keyword, Search Volume, Keyword Difficulty, Current Rank, Competitor Rank, and Opportunity Score.

## 4. Review Intelligence Engine (Phase 9)

An AI-powered module that ingests all Google Reviews and performs deep semantic analysis.
*   **Output**: 
    *   Positive & Negative Themes
    *   Overall Sentiment Score
    *   Recurring Complaints (e.g., "response time", "parking")
    *   Recurring Strengths (e.g., "friendly faculty", "great support")

## 5. Revenue Opportunity Engine (Phase 10)

A proprietary system that translates SEO metrics into financial estimates.
*   **Metrics Calculated**: Estimated missed calls, lost leads, lost visibility, and lost conversions due to current rankings and ratings.
*   **Revenue Opportunity Score**: A proprietary metric indicating the growth ceiling.
*   **Example Output**: "Improving your average rating from 4.2 to 4.6 and responding to your 34 unanswered reviews could increase your local map inquiries by 22–30%."
