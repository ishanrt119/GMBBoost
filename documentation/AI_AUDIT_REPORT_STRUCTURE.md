# AI Audit Report Structure

## 1. The 14-Section Architecture

The final output is organized into a highly structured, premium report format that serves as the dashboard view and the PDF export.

1.  **Executive Summary**: AI-generated high-level briefing.
2.  **Business Snapshot**: Cover page details and assigned Business Tier.
3.  **GBP Health Score**: The main 0-100 dial and 7-pillar breakdown.
4.  **Competitor Intelligence**: Smart matching based on >70% similarity score.
5.  **Keyword Rankings**: Current positions for top localized terms.
6.  **Local Visibility Grid**: Map-based visualization (3x3 to 7x7) powered by the Rank Tracking Provider Layer.
7.  **Review Intelligence**: AI breakdown of recurring strengths/weaknesses and sentiment.
8.  **Competitor Gap Analysis**: Head-to-head comparison against the Best Competitor.
9.  **Keyword Gap Analysis**: Uncovering missed opportunities and search volumes.
10. **Conversion Intelligence**: Financial impact estimates via the Revenue Opportunity Engine.
11. **Profile Completion**: Checklists of optimized vs. missing GBP fields.
12. **Revenue Opportunity Score**: The proprietary score indicating total growth ceiling.
13. **Growth Roadmap**: AI-generated structured action plan.
14. **Industry Benchmarking**: Contextualizing performance against market averages.

## 2. AI Growth Roadmap (Phase 11)

Replaces standard generic recommendations with a chronological execution plan.
*   **30-Day Plan**: Immediate quick wins (e.g., filling out missing categories, responding to negative reviews).
*   **60-Day Plan**: Intermediate optimizations (e.g., establishing a post cadence, Q&A seeding).
*   **90-Day Plan**: Advanced growth tactics (e.g., targeted review generation campaigns).
*   **Recommendation Schema**: Each item must include Priority, Difficulty, Expected Impact, Expected Lead/Ranking Gain, and Implementation Steps.

## 3. Industry Benchmarking (Phase 12)

Every metric in the report is visualized alongside relative benchmarks to provide immediate context to the business owner:
*   **Industry Average**
*   **Top 25% Performers**
*   **Top 10% Performers**
*   **Best Tier-Matched Competitor**

## 4. Premium PDF Report V2 (Phase 14)

The PDF export engine translates the 14 sections into a beautifully designed, white-label ready document. It utilizes heavy visualization, avoiding walls of text in favor of:
*   Radar charts for the GBP Health Score pillars.
*   Color-coded heatmaps for the Local Visibility Grid.
*   Side-by-side matrices for Competitor and Keyword Gap Analyses.
*   Clear typography and formatting that rivals enterprise tools like Grexa and BrightLocal.
